ML-199 - how long building a Flow by hand actually takes, so a redesigned Bars tab has something measured to be compared against rather than an impression that it feels quicker. Active time is wall clock with idle trimmed out: the stopwatch stops after 60s without input and whenever the page is hidden, so a session left open while the phone rang doesn't inflate the figure. Bars time is the part of that spent on the Bars tab (including the per-block inspector) - the bit the redesign is aimed at.

Per bar and per block are both shown because they answer different questions. One 16-bar block and sixteen 1-bar blocks are the same music but very different data entry: per block measures the cost of the card UI, per bar measures the cost per unit of actual music, which is the only fair way to compare flows of different lengths. Per bar is blank for edit sessions on purpose - an edit touches an unknown subset of the flow's bars, so any per-bar figure there would be arithmetic rather than a measurement.

Median leads, with min/max beside it. One interrupted run visibly moves a mean at this sample size. If min and max are miles apart, the median isn't describing anything stable yet - collect more runs before drawing a conclusion. Statistics cover completed, non-excluded sessions only; import-assisted (“Create from file”) runs are kept out of the headline and the breakdowns, and shown separately under Create vs edit.

Theory grades

What each grade in the Theory tool asks when a quiz is set to "Grade 1-5" (feature theory_grades). A draft from the ABRSM Music Theory syllabus, Grades 1-5, for checking. Grades are cumulative: Grade 3 asks everything in Grades 1-3. Each grade below lists only what it adds. The player still picks the clef (from the grade's clefs), and whether Keys shows key signatures, scales or both, and whether Notation asks names, meanings or both. Changes are made in public/theoryEngine.js. The reasoning and sources are in docs/theory-grades.md.

Not asked yet (new quiz types, ML-309 C): intervals, triads and chords, cadences, transposition, the chromatic scale, technical names of scale degrees, triplets and irregular groupings, grouping and beaming, and instruments and voices.

Shared music

Everything one member can show another: recordings and videos, documents, the pieces a band shares or that are public, a band's practice lists and its name. It is where you deal with a member's report, and where you take something down - because someone in a recording asked (the privacy policy says they can), or it is not theirs to share, or it breaks the terms. Remove takes it off every piece and deletes its file. The people it belonged to are told what happened, why and what to do next, in the app and by email; you see the message before it goes. This can't be undone. A band itself can't be removed without taking its music with it, so a name that breaks the terms is changed with Rename.

Rest messages

What the 30-second rest between practice-session blocks shows - one message at a time, with its icon (breathing ones show a breathing circle instead). Every player gets them in their own shuffled order, each once before any comes round again, never the same kind twice running, and a breathing one at least every third rest. For picks who sees it: everyone, brass players (lip messages) or brass and woodwind players (breath and air). Keep them short enough to read in a few seconds, and written for a ten-year-old. Icons are Material Symbols names. Changes show straight away - no release needed.

Notifications

Announcements shown to every account under ☰ → Notifications, with a red dot on ☰ until they've read it. Publish now or schedule a time - anyone with the app open sees it within about a minute, everyone else next time they open it. Urgent ones also pop up over whatever they're doing until they tap "Got it". Optional expiry hides it from everyone afterwards. Plain text; line breaks are kept. The "update available - reload" notice after a release is automatic and doesn't need a notification here. A band's notices - sent by its organisers, music managers or band leader to the band - are listed here too, with who sent them: withdraw one that shouldn't be there.

Flows

Export flows from this environment as MusicXML and import them into another - e.g. production → dev or sandbox for testing. One flow exports as a .musicxml file, several as a .zip. Imports always arrive as your own private flow (no band, not public) until you publish or move them yourself. Uploaded audio/video and document files aren't copied; YouTube links are. The same files open in MuseScore and other notation software.

Retention

Information about people who have stopped using the app shouldn't be kept for ever. An account nobody has used for a set time gets an email, then a second, and is then deleted - exactly as "Delete my account" does it, so the practice history stays as anonymous statistics. Using the app at any point starts the clock again. Super admin accounts are never touched. The same run clears used and expired invites after 30 days and resolved feedback after 12 months. It runs once a day; nothing happens until it is switched on here.
