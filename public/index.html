      SmartLearn

      What it does
      • Anything you get wrong comes back sooner and more often, until you get it right.
      • A question you miss comes round again a few questions later, while it's fresh.
      • It notices the ones you hesitate on, and brings those back too.
      • Things you learnt a while ago come back now and then, to check they've stuck.
      • In Theory, Your weak spots gathers everything you're still learning into a round of its own.
      • In Pitch it's the notes you miss; in Tempo, the speeds you're furthest off.
      Why it works
      • Your time goes on what you don't know yet, not on what you already do.
      • Having to remember something again, just as it starts to fade, is what makes it stay.
      • It remembers for you, round after round, so every round picks up where you left off.
